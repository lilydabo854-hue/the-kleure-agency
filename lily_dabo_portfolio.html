Why Klèure?

Derived from the Afrikaans idea of colour, Klèure represents the belief that brands should never feel flat, generic, or interchangeable.

A brand has a personality. It has a voice. It has emotions attached to it. It should make people feel something.

The problem in today's digital landscape is when every business starts sounding exactly the same. Klèure exists to bring the colour back.

Strategic Framework

How I Work

Good work starts before the first post.

Every new social media management engagement begins with one full month dedicated to strategy before the main publishing phase begins. This is intentional: the goal is to understand the brand before filling its feed.

01

Discovery

Discuss the business, audience, goals, challenges, current social presence and expectations through kickoff discussion and collection of assets.

02

Strategy Month

Dedicated first month for brand review, audience research, competitor analysis, content pillars, messaging, and calendar development.

03

Content Preparation

Once strategy is approved, prepare content ideas, captions, graphics, carousels, video edits and publishing calendar for client review.

04

Publishing Begins

Regular publishing starts after approval, scheduled according to the agreed calendar and package deliverables.

05

Monitoring & Reporting

Monitor relevant metrics including reach, views, interactions, profile activity, follows, saves, shares and clicks.

06

Refine & Repeat

Use findings to inform the next cycle. Identify what is performing, what needs adjustment and which opportunities deserve attention.

Plan with intention. Publish with purpose. Improve with evidence.
